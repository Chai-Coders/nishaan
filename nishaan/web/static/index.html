Ledger

Every decryption is a signed record committed by at least three of four independent nodes. Take nodes offline or make one malicious, then send or open a document to watch consensus route around it.

Send a document

      
The PDF is encrypted once (AES-256-GCM). Each recipient gets the key wrapped with ML-KEM-768, and a second key layer is split across the ledger nodes, so nobody can open it without leaving a record.

Open as a recipient

      
The record is signed and committed before any key is released. Only then do two ledger nodes hand over their key shares, and the copy is fingerprinted for this session.

Compare two copies

      
Two recipients' copies of the same document. Blink between them: the words sit up to 0.35 pt apart, which is about a tenth of a millimetre.

      

        

        
      

      

    


    
    

      
Trace a leak

      
Give the forensic cell a leaked file and nothing else. The investigation is itself recorded on the ledger before the original can be decrypted for comparison.

Audit the ledger

      
Play a privileged insider. Each node re-verifies its chain from genesis, nodes are cross-checked, and signed checkpoints on removable media catch even an attacker who has stolen every node key.
